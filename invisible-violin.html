How to Play:
  • Pitch Hand (Left Hand): Raise or lower your left hand. Landmark data is smoothed with a moving average buffer so notes remain solid and jitter-free.
  • Bow Hand (Right Hand): Move your right hand horizontally back and forth like drawing a violin bow across strings. Faster motion increases tone loudness and brilliance. When your bow stops, the sound fades naturally to silence.
  • Match the Note Game: Position your left hand to hit the glowing target note (e.g. D4, A4) and draw the bow. Hold the pitch steady for a moment to score points and build a streak combo!
  • Pinch to Mute: Touch your left thumb and index finger together to instantly mute the violin string.
